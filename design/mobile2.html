Elder Recollections

How to Recognize It

Soft, bright green leaves that grow rapidly after seasonal rains. Reddish stems when mature, small clustered seed heads.

Where It Grows

Near the kitchen wall, after the rain. Prefers rich disturbed soil near rainwater runoffs.

What They Told Me About Its Uses

Eaten as a green vegetable, cooked with groundnut sauce. Nourishing for convalescents and children.

format_quote

“We cook them with groundnut sauce and serve while the steam is still high.”

Field Recording Note

Preparation, As Told

Pick the leaves young before flowering. Steam gently or saute with onions, tomatoes, and crushed peanuts until tender.
